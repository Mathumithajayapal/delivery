Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer same-day delivery for bulk materials?

Yes, we offer same-day delivery for orders placed before 10:00 AM on weekdays, subject to fleet availability and distance. Pro accounts receive priority routing.

What is your return policy on tools and paint?

Unused tools in original packaging can be returned within 30 days with a receipt. Custom mixed paint is final sale, but we will gladly adjust the tint if it doesn't match your approved swatch.

How do I set up a Pro Contractor account?

You can apply online or at the Pro Desk in any of our stores. You will need to provide your business license, EIN, and basic company information. Approval usually takes 24 hours.

Do you repair tools bought from other stores?

Absolutely. Our certified repair technicians service all major brands regardless of where they were purchased. We provide free diagnostic quotes before any work begins.

Can I track my bulk delivery in real-time?

Yes! Once your delivery is dispatched, you will receive an SMS and email with a live GPS tracking link so you know exactly when the flatbed will arrive at your job site.
