Got Questions?

Booking FAQ

Are consultations completely free?

Yes! In-store material and paint consultations are completely free. If you require an on-site visit for a massive bulk delivery assessment, a minor dispatch fee may apply which is credited toward your purchase.

Do I need to bring blueprints?

While not strictly required, bringing blueprints, room measurements, or inspiration photos significantly helps our team provide accurate material quantities and exact paint color matches.

Can I drop off tools during this visit?

Absolutely. Select "Tool Repair Assessment" in the booking form. Bring your broken power tools, and our certified repair tech will run a diagnostic and provide a timeline during your session.
