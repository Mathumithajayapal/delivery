Our Origins

From a Small Shop to an Industry Leader.

BuildPro started as a family-owned corner paint shop dedicated to helping neighbors fix up their homes. Fast forward to today, we've grown into a comprehensive supply center for major contractors.

Our secret hasn't changed: we still believe in a firm handshake, honest pricing, and stocking only the most reliable brands on the market. We don't just sell tools; we supply success.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

These four pillars are the foundation of every interaction we have with our contractors, builders, and home improvers.

Uncompromising Quality

We only stock industry-tested brands. If a tool or paint formula doesn't pass our durability tests, it doesn't make it to our shelves.

Community First

We build strong partnerships with local tradesmen. When our local community succeeds in their builds, we succeed as a store.

Expert Knowledge

Our floor staff isn't just retail workers; they are former contractors, painters, and tradesmen who provide real, actionable advice.

Reliability

We know downtime costs money. We ensure tools are repaired rapidly and bulk materials are delivered exactly on schedule.
