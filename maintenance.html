How Our Repair Process Works

We hate hidden fees as much as you do. Our process is designed to keep you informed from drop-off to pickup.

1. Drop-Off

Bring your broken equipment to our service desk, no appointment necessary.

2. Diagnosis & Quote

Within 24 hours, we inspect the tool and provide a firm quote for parts and labor.

3. Expert Repair
